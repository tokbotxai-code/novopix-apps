NovoBot – privacy policy

NovoBot does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. The game has no accounts, ads, analytics, or third-party scripts and fonts.

What the game stores

Only on your device (browser local storage): completed levels, last level, score, lives and settings (sound, mode). This data is never sent anywhere. Clear the site or app data to delete it.

Permissions and children

The game requests no permissions (camera, location, contacts). It is suitable for all ages and has no ads or purchases.
